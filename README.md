Sri Mahamuni Oil Mills | Pro Billing & Analytics SystemA modern, high-performance Point of Sale (POS) and Inventory Management System specifically designed for oil mill operations. This application provides a seamless transition from product selection to finalized billing with built-in business intelligence analytics.🌟 Key Features🛒 Dynamic POS EngineDual Entry Mode: Switch instantly between "By Weight" (KG) and "By Amount" (₹).Responsive Controls: Full keyboard support. Press Enter to add items or finalize bills instantly on both PC and Mobile.Smart Cart: Real-time calculation of subtotal, discounts, and change amount.📊 Stats Center (Business Intelligence)Lifetime & Custom Range Analytics: View data from all time or filter by specific dates.KPI Dashboard: Track Total Revenue, Bill Count, Average Bill Value, and Total Volume (KG) sold.Visual Insights:Revenue Bar Chart: Identify which products generate the most cash.Weight Distribution Pie Chart: Real-time percentages of oil types moved, with high-visibility external labels.Peak Performance Tracking: Automatically identifies top-selling products and preferred payment methods.📋 Sales History & ReportingFiltered Export: Generate PDF reports that respect your active filters (Date & Payment Mode).Detailed Breakdowns: PDF exports include Item name, Quantity (kg), and Unit Price on separate lines for clarity.Management Tools: Ability to delete individual bills with a custom safety confirmation modal to keep records accurate.📦 Inventory MasterFull CRUD Support: Add, Edit, or Delete products from the central inventory.Real-time Sync: Changes in inventory prices reflect immediately in the POS selection grid.🛠️ Tech StackFrontend: HTML5, JavaScript (ES6+), Tailwind CSSIcons: Lucide-ReactCharts: Chart.js with Datalabels PluginPDF Generation: jsPDF & jsPDF-AutoTablePersistence: Browser LocalStorage for offline data management.🚀 Getting StartedClone the repository:git clone [https://github.com/your-username/mahamuni-oil-pos.git](https://github.com/your-username/mahamuni-oil-pos.git)
